HoneyBook → TaxCloud Converter

Turn a HoneyBook Payments report into a file TaxCloud can import. Everything happens in your browser. Your client data is never uploaded anywhere.

1Your business

This is where your products or services ship from, normally your shop or office. It's saved in this browser only, so you only enter it once.

2Reporting period

Leave the dates blank to include everything in the file. Project adds up every payment on a wedding into one line (fewer rows to review). Payment keeps one line per HoneyBook payment, with its own date. Ask your accountant if you're not sure which date to use.

3HoneyBook Payments report

4Venue addresses

TaxCloud needs a ship-to address on every row: street, city, state and 5-digit ZIP, all four. HoneyBook doesn't include it, so type each venue in below (yellow rows are incomplete) or load your venue list. A list with one row per venue works: pick the venue for each project from the dropdown and the address fills in. Your choices are remembered in this browser.

Advanced options

Customer IDs are created automatically from each client's name, so there is nothing to set up.

5Download

Upload the first file to TaxCloud. The review copy has three extra columns for your own comparison. TaxCloud will reject it, so don't upload that one.

Open source: formats data only, doesn't give tax advice. Check results against your TaxCloud account before filing.
